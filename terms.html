Terms & Conditions

Version 2026-10 · Effective October 2026

Pre-launch draft. These terms describe the present prototype and approval-only service. Have the final terms reviewed and add administrator contact details before opening registrations broadly.

1. Using Roavely

Roavely lets approved travelers explore destinations, suggest and vote on trips, discuss activities, make itineraries, estimate costs and store certain travel details. You must provide accurate account information, use your own account and comply with applicable law. Registration does not guarantee access: administrators approve, decline or suspend accounts at their discretion for legitimate operational and safety reasons.

2. Account verification and approval

New accounts must confirm their email address and accept the current terms. Pending accounts cannot access shared trip data. Approval is required before participation. Administrator permissions are assigned by the site owner; choosing a role or editing your own profile cannot give you administrator access.

3. Trip groups and invitations

Invited group members can see the shared information associated with their trip, including suggestions, votes, comments, and itinerary details. Avoid publishing passwords, financial credentials, passport numbers, medical details, or other sensitive documents in shared content. You are responsible for checking who joins a group and for what you share.

4. Content and behavior

You retain ownership of the original photos, messages and other content you create. By sharing content with an approved trip group, you authorize Roavely to display it to that group to operate the service. You must have rights to content you upload. Harassment, threats, illegal material, impersonation, spam, credential harvesting and attempts to evade access restrictions are prohibited. Content may be removed when necessary for safety or compliance.

5. Estimates, bookings and travel advice

Travel prices, budgets, suggestions, arrival times, packing lists and other guidance can be approximations. Roavely does not guarantee fares, availability, travel safety, entry permission or booking completion. Check official sources, actual carriers and accommodation providers before purchasing or traveling. The prototype is not a travel agency and does not process travel bookings or payments.

6. Payments and expense records

Expense-sharing tools are records and calculators only. Marking a balance paid does not move money, confirm a bank transaction, or establish legally enforceable debt.

7. Privacy and storage

Account email addresses, verification status, access decisions and approved-group collaboration data are stored in Roavely's separate Supabase project. Browser-stored prototype data such as local images, checklists and some expense records may remain only on your device and may disappear if browser data is erased. Roavely uses your device's location only when a location-based function is explicitly requested; related features may open an external map provider. Never treat local-only storage as a backup.

8. Availability and security

Roavely is evolving and may be unavailable, changed, or interrupted. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no software or internet-based service can guarantee uninterrupted access or absolute security. Keep independent copies of important trip reservations and photos.

9. Ending access and changes

You may stop using Roavely at any time. Contact the administrator to request account review or deletion. The administrator may suspend access for violations or security concerns. If material terms change, you may be asked to review and accept the new version before using collaborative services again.

10. Contact and questions

For help with access, account decisions, or data deletion, contact the Roavely project administrator through the official project support channel once published. The support contact will be supplied before the service is opened to the general public.
